# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension ("the Extension") collects, uses, and protects your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our extension.

## Information Collection and Usage

### Data We Collect

The Extension operates entirely within your browser and **does not store or collect any persistent information**. All processing happens in real-time and remains in your browser's memory:

- **Widget and Component Information**: When you use the devtools panel, the Extension temporarily analyzes widgets and components visible on the current web page. This information is processed in real-time to populate the devtools panel display and is not stored.
- **Page Context**: Information about the current web page is accessed temporarily to provide devtools functionality but is never stored or transmitted.

### How We Use Your Information

- To provide real-time devtools functionality
- To display widget information and component details in the devtools panel
- **That's it.** No data is stored, analyzed, or used for any other purpose.

## Data Storage and Security

- **No Data Storage**: The Extension does not store any information on your computer or in the cloud. All analysis happens in real-time while the Extension is running.
- **No External Communication**: No data is ever transmitted to external servers, databases, or third-party services.
- **No Tracking or Analytics**: We do not track your usage, collect telemetry, or use analytics tools.
- **Memory Only**: When you close the devtools panel or browser tab, all processed information is immediately discarded.

## Permissions

The Extension requests the following browser permissions:

- **Content Script Permissions**: To access and analyze web page content
- **DevTools API Access**: To integrate with browser developer tools
- **Storage Access**: To store Extension settings and preferences locally

These permissions are used solely to provide the Extension's functionality.

## User Control

You have full control over the Extension:

- You can uninstall the Extension at any time through your browser's extension management interface
- You can disable specific permissions in your browser settings
- You can clear all stored Extension data through your browser's settings

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the date at the bottom of this policy.
